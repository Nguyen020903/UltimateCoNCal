Damage Calculation System

How Damage Is Calculated

  1. Unit Weight: Each unit has a weight value that determines how much damage it absorbs in a stack.
  2. Armor Type: Units are classified as "soft" (infantry) or "hard" (vehicles, armor) and take damage accordingly.
  3. RNG Factor: A random factor (80-120%) is applied to final damage.
  4. Overkill Rules: Units can only take ~33% of their HP in damage unless specific conditions are met.
  5. HP Scaling: Units deal less damage as their HP decreases.
  6. Ranged Combat: Artillery and naval units attack without receiving return damage.

Terrain Effects

  • Urban/Forest: Infantry units get attack and defense bonuses.
  • Mountains: Armored vehicles suffer penalties.
  • Water: Only naval units can operate effectively.

Stack Effects

  • Overstacking: Exceeding 10 ground units or 5 air/naval units reduces effectiveness.
  • Mixed Units: Combining different unit types can maximize combat effectiveness.
