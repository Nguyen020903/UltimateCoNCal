terrain rules:
1 = in flight (air unit only)
0 = on the ground (Air unit only)
10 = open ground
12 = mountains
13 = forest
14 = urban
21 = suburban
15 = jungle
16 = tundra
17 = desert
19 = high sea
20 = coastal water

attack and defense type: 
0 = soft target (infantry, support vehicles)
4 = hard target (armored vehicles, bunkers)
1 = fixed wing target (aircraft, planes)
8 = rotary wing target (helicopters)
1004 = drones (UAVs)
1003 = missiles (cruise missiles, ballistic missiles)
2 = surface vessel (naval ships)
5 = submarine
3 = building (structures, facilities)
7 = population (civilians)


resource rules:
1 = supply
2 = component
5 = fuel
6 = electronic
4 = rare material 
3 = manpower
20 = money 


unit level and tier (infantry only)
basic < advanced < modern 
file name that contain number means + 1 level 
for example basic = level 1
            basic(1) = level 2
            advanced = level 3
            advanced(1) = level 4
            advanced(2) = level 5
            modern = level 6

notes: not all units have the same number of levels

doctrine effects:
Eastern - focuses on quantity and cost-effectiveness
Western - focuses on quality and technology
European - balanced approach with territorial focus

stack mechanics:
- Units can be stacked in the same province
- Stack limit of 10 units per stack
- Overstacking penalty applies when units exceed stack limit
- Stack composition should consider complementary units (anti-air with ground forces)

special conditions:
- Entrenchment: Defensive bonus increases over time for stationary units
- Officer boost: Officers provide combat bonuses to nearby units
- Terrain effects: Different unit types perform better in specific terrains
- Weather and time of day can affect combat performance
